# Undead Afterdark Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Undead Afterdark does not require an account and does not intentionally collect or sell personal information.

## Local game data
Solo progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. This data stays on the device unless you remove the app or the operating system clears its storage.

## Online co-op
If you use online co-op, network services required to establish the connection may process technical connection information such as IP addresses, device/network information, room codes, and connection timing. This information is used to provide multiplayer connectivity and is not used by the developer to build advertising profiles.

## Advertising and purchases
The current version does not contain advertising or live in-app purchases. If advertising or purchases are added in a future App Store release, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that release.

## Analytics
The current version does not include developer-operated analytics or behavioral tracking.

## Children
Undead Afterdark is a zombie action game and is not directed to young children.

## Changes
This policy may be updated when game features or third-party services change. The latest version will remain available in this repository.

## Contact and support
For support or privacy questions, use the public support page in this repository or open an issue in the Undead Afterdark GitHub repository.
